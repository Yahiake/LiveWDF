# Fairchild 2N4403

Soundcraft ED2162 issue 8 uses two 2N4403 PNPs in its input stage. The native 2N4403 card uses the Fairchild LTspice transfer parameters, including forward Early effect, base-emitter recombination and high-current gain roll-off. Junction capacitance, temperature variation and package resistances are outside this static reduction. MiSTer-Discrete's 2N4403 reference uses constant β = 150 and its complementary 2N4401's saturation current. The original Fairchild parameter set gives an independent device-specific fit for this input pair.

The `[2n4403]` test forces 0.1, 1 and 10 mA at 1 V, and 150 mA at 2 V in the full ngspice model. It compares native collector current and base-emitter voltage with that solution and checks the onsemi gain limits at all four points. The full reference includes 10 Ω base and 0.715 Ω collector resistance. No extra iteration or state is required beyond the existing opt-in Gummel–Poon transport law.
