# Additional pedal listening examples

| Clip | Settings |
|---|---|
| [Boss GE-7 Mid Scoop](audio/Boss-GE-7-Mid-Scoop.mp3) | 400 Hz 0.22, 800 Hz 0.08, 1.6 kHz 0.25. Remaining sliders and Level centered. Approximate maximum cut of 8.1 dB at 800 Hz relative to the flat pedal. |
| [Boss PQ-4 Mid Sweep](audio/Boss-PQ-4-Mid-Sweep.mp3) | Middle Gain 0.87, Middle Frequency 0.10 → 0.90 → 0.10. Remaining controls centered. The boosted band travels from approximately 125 Hz to 1 kHz and back. |
| [Boss DM-2 Original vs Revised](audio/Boss-DM-2-Original-vs-Revised.mp3) | Fixed input level and factory knobs. Original model first, revised compander second. |
| [DOD 250 Gray Dry/Wet](audio/DOD-250-Gray-Dry-Wet.mp3) | Gain and Level 0.73. Includes the measured effect of the LM741 stage and output diode clipper. |

The EQ clips use level-matched dry and wet halves. The wet half starts at 0:05.01. All sources are Guitar-TECHS direct inputs. The DOD uses PF300 palm mutes, the EQs use PF300 major chords, and the DM-2 uses Sire T7 performance 11. See [recording credits and license](README.md#recording-credits).
