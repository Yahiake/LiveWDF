# Factory amp power stages

The factory presets model one dry signal path per channel selection. Tube circuits use the phase splitter, output valves, feedback where fitted, and a resistive speaker load through the output transformer. Most amp supplies use fixed operating rails derived from the service drawings. Transformer parasitics are estimates. Cabinet IRs supply frequency-dependent speaker and microphone coloration for playback.

## Crunch amps

- **Fender Tweed Deluxe 5E3** uses the original 12AX7 cathodyne and 6V6GT pair with the shared 250 Ω cathode resistor and 25 µF bypass. The 370 V plate and 320 V screen supplies come from Fender's later '57 Deluxe service drawing. Hammond's 1750E specifies 8.5 kΩ plate to plate into 8 Ω. [5E3 drawing](https://www.prowessamplifiers.com/schematics/fender/deluxe_5e3_schem.pdf) · [Fender ’57 Deluxe service drawing](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-57-Deluxe-Schematic.pdf) · [Hammond transformer data](https://www.hammfg.com/electronics/transformers/guitar/deluxe-5e3)
- **Marshall 1959 and 2203** use the 1981 Lead Series splitter and 100 W EL34 output stage. The Presence circuit receives the 4 Ω feedback tap, which also carries the modeled load. Primary impedance is estimated at 1.7 kΩ plate to plate. The 2203 transformer takes the Hammond 1750U replacement's 8.85 H primary, 7.97 mH leakage and 31.9 Ω primary resistance, with 0.16 Ω for the 4 Ω tap estimated as half the 0.32 Ω full secondary. [Hammond 1750U](https://www.hammfg.com/files/parts/pdf/h1750U.pdf) · [Marshall phase splitter](https://www.drtube.com/schematics/marshall/jcm800pr.gif) · [Marshall 100 W power stage](https://www.drtube.com/schematics/marshall/jcm800pw.gif)
- **Vox AC15 No. 3** uses the OA/031 ECC83 splitter, Cut circuit and shared-cathode EL84 pair. The drawing marks the main rail at 310 V and the output transformer at 8 kΩ plate to plate. The load uses the documented 8 Ω tap. [Jennings OA/031](https://www.drtube.com/schematics/vox/ac15-oa031.gif)
- **Vox AC30 Top Boost** uses the OS/065 ECC83 splitter, 250 kΩ Cut and shared 50 Ω cathode circuit for the EL84 quartet, including the 4 kΩ transformer and 16 Ω equivalent speaker load. The 320 V rectified rail follows OS/010. [Jennings OS/065](https://www.drtube.com/schematics/vox/ac30-6-os065.gif) · [Jennings OS/010](https://www.drtube.com/schematics/vox/ac30-tbmod-os010.gif)
- **Orange OR120 Graphic MkII** follows the January 1974 common-cathode feedback driver, direct-coupled cathodyne and EL34 quartet. The Boost circuit is on the feedback driver's cathode. The 15 Ω tap supplies the feedback and model load. The transformer primary impedance is estimated at 1.75 kΩ plate to plate. [Orange 1974 service drawing](https://el34world.com/charts/Schematics/files/orange/orange_mkii1974.pdf)
- **Orange Rockerverb 50 Mk I** shares the same 2004 ECC83 splitter and ORA-CD206 6V6 output board on both channels. The model takes the dry loop feed with the damping connection closed. The 400 V plate supply, 395 V screen supply and 4 kΩ:8 Ω transformer are estimates based on the 290 V transformer secondary and the fixed-bias four-valve output stage. [Orange ORA-CD204 and ORA-CD206](https://civade.com/AudioPro/Orange/orange_rockerverb_guitar_amplifier_schematic.pdf)

## High-gain amps

- **EVH 5150III 100S Stealth** uses the red channel with four 6L6GCs, the 8 Ω tap, Presence and Resonance. [FMIC 7700254000 service supplement, p. 8](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3573472/Evh-5150iii-100s.html?page=8)
- **Dual Rectifier** uses the two-channel RP-1F four-6L6GC stage into 8 Ω. Orange/Vintage Presence acts on the power feedback loop through C51. Red/Modern opens that feedback loop and uses preamp Presence. The phase inverter uses the 82 kΩ signal-side and 90 kΩ reference-side plate loads from the drawing. The domestic Bold transformer secondary feeds four 1N4007s or two hot 5U4GBs through the two ganged rectifier switch poles. The two reservoir capacitors, balancing resistors, choke, B+ filters and domestic 6L6 bias supply follow page 4. The 1N4007 junction capacitances are shown explicitly at their datasheet nominal. The winding resistance and choke model are estimates. Preamp-only playback uses the factory fixed rails for the isolated signal path. ## Solid-state and hybrid amps

The reduced JC-120 and Valvestate stages model gain, feedback, and rail clipping with linear output resistance. Crossover distortion and current limiting are outside their model scope.

- **ENGL E530** routes the normalled loop through the level buffer and the built-in TBA820M practice amp into 4 Ω. The IC model uses centered 9 V rails, measured-spec swing and gain, and the external gain and speaker coupling capacitors. [ENGL service drawing](https://schematicheaven.net/bargainbin/engl_e530.pdf) · [ENGL manual](https://www.engl-amps.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/om_e530.pdf) · [ST TBA820M datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/tba820m.pdf)
- **Roland JC-120** follows the 1979 AP113 power circuit, including the 47 kΩ voltage feedback, 1 kΩ current-feedback leg, output choke and 8 Ω speaker load. A reduced class-AB stage models the active circuitry and rails. The choke inductance is estimated. [Roland fourth-edition service notes](https://www.synfo.nl/servicemanuals/Roland/ROLAND_JC-120_JC-160_SERVICE_NOTES_4th.pdf)
- **Marshall Valvestate 8100** uses the PC0689P input coupling and the speaker voltage/current-sense network. A rail-limited class-AB equivalent represents the IC8B drive and complementary Darlington output on estimated ±36 V rails with a 4 Ω load. [Marshall PC0689P power board](https://www.drtube.com/schematics/marshall/8080-pwr.gif)

The **Fender Tweed Champ 5F1** uses its existing single-ended 6V6 circuit and 4 Ω load. [Fender 5F1 service drawing](https://schematicheaven.net/fenderamps/champ_5f1_schem.pdf)
